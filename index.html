Help & frequently asked questions

The app will not open or respond

Close iPOTION and reopen it. If the issue continues, restart your iPhone and check that iOS is up to date.

Something does not look or work right

Try closing and reopening the app. When reporting the issue, include your iPhone model, iOS version and the steps that led to it.

How do I report a bug?

Use the Contact support link and describe what happened, what you expected to happen, and whether you can reproduce the issue.

Purchases and billing

App Store purchases and billing are managed by Apple. For a billing or refund request, use Apple’s official Report a Problem service.

Need a hand?

We welcome questions, bug reports and suggestions. Please do not include passwords, payment details or other sensitive information in a support request.
